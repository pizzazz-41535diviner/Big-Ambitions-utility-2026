## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is an indie business and life simulation game developed by Hovgaard Games. It entered Early Access on March 10, 2023, and reached full release on August 28, 2026. Set in an open-world version of New York City, it combines character needs, property management, transportation, retail operations, logistics, employment, and business expansion.

**This tool** is a comprehensive utility for **Big Ambitions**. ### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Money** — Provides a large or frozen cash balance for purchasing property, vehicles, furniture, inventory, services, and business upgrades.
- **Freeze Personal Needs** — Prevents the character's needs from decreasing, making it easier to manage businesses without constant interruptions for rest and personal care.

- **Fast Movement** — Increases character movement speed when traveling between properties, businesses, suppliers, and other city locations.
- **Vehicle Assistance** — Provides optional support for driving and transportation tasks when moving products between wholesalers, warehouses, and businesses.
- **Inventory Quantity** — Helps maintain available product quantities while handling store stock, warehouse inventory, and supply deliveries.
- **Business Cash Flow** — Supports business expansion by reducing the financial pressure of rent, payroll, inventory purchases, vehicles, and other operating expenses.
- **Employee Management** — Provides optional assistance while hiring, training, scheduling, and maintaining employees across stores, warehouses, offices, and factories.

- **Combat Support** — Big Ambitions does not have a combat system, so the utility includes no combat enhancements or combat-related functions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the utility before entering the game and activate movement options only after the character is fully loaded into the city. Fast movement is useful when visiting suppliers, collecting products, checking businesses, or traveling between properties. Disable the option before entering menus, vehicles, or loading screens if movement behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

Use inventory assistance when transporting products, filling store shelves, or supplying warehouses. Product handling is central to Big Ambitions: businesses require stock, warehouses can centralize supplies, and vehicles and employees can support deliveries. Do not edit quantities during an active purchase, delivery, or restocking operation.

### World & Environment

Big Ambitions uses an open city environment where the character can enter properties, rent or purchase locations, furnish businesses and homes, drive vehicles, and build an expanding company network. Money and time options are especially useful for testing new locations, experimenting with store equipment, and comparing business strategies without restarting the campaign.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat in Big Ambitions. Progression focuses on personal needs, work, business management, property, inventory, employees, logistics, transportation, and financial growth rather than fighting or character combat.
